How ordering works

  1. Build it

    Choose your khata or paper above. The price works itself out as you go.

  2. Add it to the memo

    Khatas, paper and ring khatas can all go on one memo.

  3. Send the memo

    Add your name and mobile number, copy the memo and send it to us.

    We reply with the final price and the delivery date. For school khatas you see the cover design before printing.

Good to know

Questions and answers

What is the smallest order?

School khatas: 100 pieces. A4 paper: one carton (5 reams). Ring khatas: a single piece.

Who designs the cover?

You give us the school's name, address and logo; we lay out the cover and show it to you. Nothing is printed until you approve it.

What does three rulings in one khata mean?

The first third is Bangla rule, the middle third English four-line and the last third maths blocks, so a small child carries one khata instead of three.

Can I buy A4 paper one ream at a time?

No, paper is supplied by the carton. One carton holds 5 reams, which is 2,500 sheets.

What can go on a ring khata's cover?

A title or subject, and under it your name, roll or batch. If you want a different subject on each khata in a pack, say so when you send the memo.

Are the prices here final?

They are estimates. The final price comes with our reply to your memo, once we have seen the quantity, the paper and where it is going.
